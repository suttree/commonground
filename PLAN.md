# Pilot website build plan

Build a small website that helps us recruit five organisations with real needs, collect interest from people, and make introductions ourselves.

The test is whether describing the work and its purpose gives both sides a better reason to talk.

## 1. Build one landing page with two routes

Explain the offer: "Find people through what needs doing and why it matters."

Give visitors two actions: "I need someone" and "I'm interested in helping."

Explain that this is an early pilot. We will personally review submissions and suggest introductions.

Use Monika's apple juice example to show what we mean by purpose. Organisations can make the same product while serving different needs, such as reducing food waste, supporting local production, or employing people who have been out of work for a long time.

## 2. Build the organisation form

Collect:

- Organisation name, website and contact details.
- What they need someone to accomplish, with an option for "We need help figuring this out."
- Who benefits from the work, and how.
- Whether they need someone to establish an approach or improve an existing operation.
- Type of engagement, budget, timing and location requirements.

Allow uncertainty in the answers. We can clarify the brief in a conversation.

## 3. Build the person form

Collect:

- Name and contact details.
- Problems they care about and people they'd like their work to serve.
- One example of relevant work, including their contribution.
- Whether they prefer figuring things out, improving something established, or both.
- Availability, preferred engagement and pay expectations.
- An optional link to existing work or a profile.

No CV upload or public profile is needed.

## 4. Set up the manual introduction process

Save submissions somewhere private that Monika, Sarper and Duncan can review. Track these states:

- Received.
- Needs a conversation.
- Ready for introductions.
- Introduced.
- Outcome recorded.

Contact each side with a short explanation of a possible match. Get agreement before sharing contact details.

After the introduction, ask whether they spoke, whether it was relevant, and what happens next.

## 5. Publish and run the first round

Check both submission flows on mobile and desktop, add a clear privacy notice, and deploy.

Invite five organisations from our existing network with current, funded needs, then recruit people relevant to those needs.

The main question after this round is: "Did the challenge, purpose and working-style information lead to a worthwhile conversation that might otherwise have been missed?"

## Definition of done

The build is complete when someone can:

- Understand the offer.
- Submit either form.
- Receive a clear next-step message.
- Appear in our private review list.

Accounts, searchable profiles, messaging and AI matching can wait until the introductions show us what would help.
